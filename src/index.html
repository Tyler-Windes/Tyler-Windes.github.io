THE BACKGROUND

Business first.
Systems with a purpose.

Managing Amkha’s operations and finances taught me to look past an idealized workflow: what happens when information is missing, priorities change or someone needs an answer? Customer needs, vendor commitments and operating constraints shaped those decisions.

My independent technical work builds on that operating perspective. The three public samples in Selected Work use fictional scenarios and synthetic data to make the implementation, decisions and test evidence inspectable. They are not Amkha deployments or paid client implementations.

INDEPENDENT SYSTEMS WORK

AI and information workflows I build, operate and improve.

I develop Python and PowerShell workflows that turn documents, recordings and data into structured, reviewable information. I select tools, models and APIs, review and modify implementations, investigate failures and verify outputs. The work includes systems I maintain and tools delivered to other users.

  • Processing and automation. Document extraction, OCR, transcription and structured outputs, with source checks, failure handling and recoverable updates.
  • Tools and technical guidance for other users. Document and photography tools in continued use, plus advice on AI backends, model selection, memory use and API setup for a released mobile app.
  • Controlled agent coordination. Defined responsibilities, scope-specific permissions, structured exchanges and verified handoffs across AI tools and agents.

This independent work spans requirements, implementation, testing, troubleshooting and user guidance. I keep important decisions and changes traceable so the systems can be reviewed, maintained and improved.

The public work samples below provide separate, inspectable examples of my requirements, data-quality and integration-reliability methods.

01 / FEATURED CASEFictional implementation exercise

Implementation Readiness Workspace

“Done” isn't the same as ready to hand over.

A service-request process can look complete while responsibility, acceptance and support handoff are still unclear. I modeled the path from intake and requirements through Jira/Confluence workflows, testing, corrections and readiness.

I kept Validation separate from Done and preserved failed tests until the corrections had been retested.
